# nap - Nice Audio Player

This app is a simple Audio player. It follows the same design principles and a compatible asthetic ethos as it's sibling media player projects:

- `npr` Omarchy friendly, minimal PDF reader. ~/code/npr
- `nvp` Omarchy friendly, minimal sleek video player. ~/code/nvp


## Unique Identity

The unique aspect of an audio player is that there IS no visual identity to audio media. Because of this, this app will present a heavier UI identity than the other apps in order to maintain the 'tactile' nature of media.

The interface is a hybrid of the minimal omarchy style with a skeuomorphic style to mimic a cassette tape player.

The "player" will appear to have a cassette mounted. The cassette tape itself is "labeled" with the file name. That might even be an ideal location for the timeline scrubber?

The cassette has the two spinning spool gears (serving no practical purpose but a bit of fun!) And of course they don't spin when not playing.

The cassette also has that distinctive ridge along the bottom - I'll leave it to you if you can think of anything for that.

Player controls are big square buttons along the bottom, arranged in typical cassette tape player order. Includes play/pause, stop, rewind, fast forward (single tap for 5 second jump, hold for continuous movement)

Also - I think a "cli-amp style basic audio wave form visualizer in between the spinning gears (where the little plastic window normally is) would be a really fun touch!

You certainly have some creative license here, so show me what you can do! Show off and have fun with your design skills. Only make sure you find an elegant balance between a skeumorphic feel and something what won't look too out of place in Omarchy.

## keys

Infer sensible ones, taking your cue from the other apps. 

- '?' for help
- '<space>' for play/pause
- 'b' for bookmark, etc...
- 'q' for quit

Also add any obvious --cli flags you think are needed.
